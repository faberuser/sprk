# Hero birthday stories (Memory Archive > monthly Orvel stories)

The birthday cutscenes from October 2019 onward (`PlainAsset/Text/CutScene/Calendar`
and `Hidden/Event/Calendar`: Oct–Dec 2019, Taily, Shakmeh, Gremory, Lucikiel,
Estelle) shipped their dialog only in English. Every other language showed the
raw text key (for example `E_C19_Dec_Esker01_28`).

These patches add SPRK's own translations of those 2,004 lines for Korean,
Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, French, German and Russian. They
are not official text: names, places and recurring terms follow the game's
existing localization (for example 이정표의 동반자, オディッチ, Pont stellaire),
and character speech styles follow their official lines where known (Taily's
lisp, Rephy's third person, Nia's broken speech). Keys a language already had
are not touched.
